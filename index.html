How I Work

AI speeds up the work. Research and judgment still lead it.

I use seven connected activities as a toolkit, not a fixed sequence, scaled to how much is unknown. AI is built into each one to speed up synthesis, exploration and prototyping, a practice I developed through LPL's Copilot and Cursor training and my own work with Claude, ChatGPT and Figma Make.

01Frame02Discover03Synthesize04Explore05Validate06Deliver & Launch07Measure & Iterate
Read about my process →
See where AI fits in each step
FrameChatGPT · Claude

Summarize requirements and stakeholder input; draft problem statements, assumptions, and success metrics for the team to align on.

DiscoverChatGPT · Claude

Synthesize interview notes, analytics, and existing research; surface patterns, gaps, and open questions to probe with users.

SynthesizeClaude · FigJam

Cluster findings into themes; draft journeys, workflows, and opportunity areas to refine with the team in FigJam.

ExploreChatGPT · Claude · Figma

Generate concept directions and edge cases; design and compare information architecture and task flows in Figma.

ValidateFigma Make · Cursor · Claude

Build interactive prototypes quickly; pre-review usability so sessions with stakeholders and real users focus on the riskiest assumptions.

Deliver & LaunchFigma · Cursor

Prepare specs, states, and content; build coded components in Cursor so engineering starts from a working reference.

Measure & IterateChatGPT · Claude

Analyze task success, adoption, feedback, and business KPIs; turn findings into priorities for the next cycle.

About

I'm a hands-on UX leader with 15+ years of experience across complex enterprise workflows and the people who depend on them, from procurement teams to quality analysts and collections agents. I lead through research, systems thinking and cross-functional alignment, shaping product direction while staying close to the design details.

My approach starts with systems thinking: mapping how fragmented tools and data actually flow through a business before redesigning the experience around the roles that use them. I've led UX strategy end to end, from research and information architecture through the design systems that keep multi-product platforms cohesive as they scale.
